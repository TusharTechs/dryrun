# DryRun — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 5 September 2026**

DryRun is a rehearsal app for hard conversations. What you type is about a
real colleague, so this policy is written to be read, not to be survived.

There is no account. We never ask for your name, email, or phone number.

## What stays on your phone

All of it, except what has to be sent to generate a reply. Specifically, these
never leave your device:

- The conversation you set up — who you're talking to, what they're like, what
  you have to say, and when it is
- Every rehearsal transcript
- Your scores, hedge counts, and before/after comparisons
- Which daily line you've done

Deleting the app deletes all of it. **Delete everything** inside the app does
the same thing immediately. There is no cloud backup, no export, and no way
for us to recover any of it — including for you.

## What is sent, and why

To make the other person reply in character and to score a run, the app sends
our server the description of the counterpart, the situation, and the messages
in the current rehearsal. Our server forwards them to the AI provider that
generates the reply, and passes the reply back.

**We do not store any of it.** That content exists only for the seconds it
takes to answer the request. It is not written to any database, not logged,
and not used to train anything.

## What our server does keep

Two things, both to stop the service being abused:

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| A random device identifier and access token | So the app can talk to the server without an account | 180 days |
| A count of requests, kept against your token and your IP address | To enforce a daily limit | 48 hours |

The device identifier is a random number generated on your phone. It is not
derived from your device's hardware, advertising ID, or anything about you,
and it identifies nobody. Your IP address is visible to our server the way it
is to every website you visit; we keep only a request count against it, and it
expires after 48 hours.

## What we don't do

- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No third-party analytics or attribution SDKs
- We do not sell or share your data with anyone, for any purpose
- We do not build a profile of you
- Nothing you type is used to train an AI model

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your phone itself. Setting one sends
nothing to us. You can decline notifications and the app works fine.

## Subscriptions

If DryRun offers a paid plan, purchases are handled entirely by the Google
Play Store or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

## Children

DryRun is for workplace conversations and is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights

Because everything personal lives on your phone, you exercise most of your
rights directly: **Delete everything** in the app erases it. For the request
counts and token held on our server, contact us and we'll delete them; they
expire on their own within 180 days regardless.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will change and the
new version will be published here before it takes effect.
